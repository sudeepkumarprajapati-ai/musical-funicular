ACME PRECISION FASTENERS — QUALITY MANUAL
Section 4.2 — Incoming Inspection Sampling Plans (Attributes)

The following sampling plan applies for General Inspection Level II. Locate the
row matching the lot size, then read the sample size and the Acceptance (Ac) /
Rejection (Re) defect counts under the applicable AQL column. A lot is rejected
if the number of nonconforming units found in the sample is greater than or
equal to Re.

Lot Size Range | Sample Size (n) | AQL 1.0% Ac/Re | AQL 1.5% Ac/Re | AQL 2.5% Ac/Re
51-90          | 13              | 0/1            | 1/2            | 1/2
91-130         | 20              | 1/2            | 1/2            | 2/3
131-200        | 32              | 1/2            | 1/2            | 2/3
201-280        | 32              | 1/2            | 2/3            | 3/4
281-500        | 50              | 2/3            | 3/4            | 5/6

Notes:
- "Nonconforming" means the unit fails any inspected characteristic called out
  on the applicable drawing.
- Sample units are to be selected at approximately regular intervals across the
  full lot per Work Instruction WI-221.
- This table applies to attribute (pass/fail) sampling. Dimensional
  characteristics are evaluated against the tolerance stated on the applicable
  engineering drawing.
