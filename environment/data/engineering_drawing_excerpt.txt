ENGINEERING DRAWING EXCERPT — DWG-FX220-C, Rev C
Part: FX-220 Fixture Bracket
Material: 6061-T6 Aluminum, per spec AMS-QQ-A-250/11

Feature: Mounting Hole Pattern (4x diameter 6.5 mm holes, Datum A|B|C established)

Note 12 (Position Tolerance): Each hole location shall be within a true position
tolerance of diameter 0.25 mm relative to Datum A|B|C, evaluated Regardless of
Feature Size (RFS) — no bonus tolerance applies.

True position is computed from the measured deviation of the actual hole center
from its nominal (X,Y) location:

    True Position (TP) = 2 x sqrt(dx^2 + dy^2)

where dx and dy are the measured deviations, in millimetres, from nominal in the
X and Y directions respectively.

A hole is conforming only if TP <= 0.25 mm.

Note 7 (Material Traceability): All raw material used for parts on this drawing
shall be traceable to a certified heat lot. A material certification matching
the heat lot actually used in production must accompany each shipment. Parts
produced from a heat lot without a matching certification are non-conforming
regardless of dimensional results.

Note 15: Surface finish, chamfer, and thread callouts on this drawing are not
subject to incoming sampling inspection under standard receiving protocol
(inspected at final assembly instead).
